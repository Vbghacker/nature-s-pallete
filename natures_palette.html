Nature's Palette

Explore the colors, stories and living systems of our planet — then use creativity and compassion to help protect them.

Start Exploring

🌿 Explore Nature

Go beyond simple facts. Discover how ecosystems connect, how art can build empathy, and how everyday choices can become acts of care.

🐝

Pollinators

Bees, butterflies and other pollinators help many flowering plants reproduce. Gardens with native flowers can provide useful habitat and food.

🦜

Wildlife Connections

Species are linked through food webs, shelter and nutrient cycles. A change to one part of an ecosystem can affect many others.

🌱

Small Habitats Matter

Even a balcony planter, school garden or patch of native plants can provide resources for insects and birds.

Try this: Look around your neighborhood and identify three different living things. Ask: what does each one need to survive?
🌲

More Than Trees

Forests are communities of plants, animals, fungi and microorganisms. They also influence water movement, soil health and local climate.

💧

Water Guardians

Healthy forests can slow rainfall, reduce erosion and help regulate water flowing into streams and rivers.

🦥

Homes for Life

Different layers — canopy, understory, forest floor and roots — create many different habitats.

🐠

Living Blue

Oceans support enormous biodiversity and help regulate Earth's climate through the movement of heat and carbon.

🪸

Coral Communities

Coral reefs provide habitat for many marine species. They are sensitive to warming and other environmental pressures.

♻️

Plastic Choices

Reducing unnecessary single-use plastics and disposing of waste correctly can help reduce litter reaching waterways.

🌡️

What Is Climate?

Weather describes short-term conditions; climate describes patterns over longer periods. Human activities are increasing greenhouse-gas concentrations and warming the planet.

🚲

Everyday Actions

Walking, cycling, sharing rides, reducing waste and using energy thoughtfully are examples of lower-impact choices.

💡

Think Systems

The biggest environmental improvements often come from many people and institutions changing systems together.

🎨

Art Makes Us Notice

Drawing, photography, music and storytelling can turn an environmental issue into something personal, visible and memorable.

👀

Look Closely

Try observing one natural object for five minutes. Notice shapes, textures, sounds and colors without disturbing it.

🖼️

Art With a Message

A poster can combine an image, a short fact and one practical action to encourage caring behavior.

💚

Compassion for Living Things

Compassion means noticing the needs of people, animals and ecosystems and responding with care rather than harm.

🤝

Care Together

Environmental care can include helping classmates, sharing resources, respecting public spaces and protecting habitats.

🌏

From Feeling to Action

Use an emotion — wonder, concern, gratitude or hope — as a starting point for one constructive action.
